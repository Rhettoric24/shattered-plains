# Existing Observation --- Market

## 2026-08 --- Developed account

### Observation

On a newer-but-not-fully-new account, Market level 4 or 5 appeared to
make Spheres substantially less constrained until large sinks such as
bulk Ghostblood recruitment.

A maxed account with roughly 300,000 Spheres could spend essentially all
of it on very large Spy/Ghostblood purchases.

### Reaction

Sphere costs began to feel less meaningful. Bulk Ghostblood purchasing
also conflicted with their intended rare-elite fantasy.

### Hypothesis

Two issues may be separate: 1. Market income becomes comfortable too
early. 2. Ghostbloods function as a bulk late-game sink despite a rarity
fantasy.

New Fabrial costs may alter this.

### Action

Do not rebalance yet. Run a fresh account and record when purchases stop
competing meaningfully.

# Existing Observation --- Readiness Audit

## 2026-08 --- New-player readiness

### Observation

Audit found a new player could recruit, compare buildings, compose
armies, understand mission risk, and make meaningful choices without
creator explanation. No P0 blocker remained after preventing
zero-operative espionage launches.

### Hypothesis

Small contextual hints may provide more value than another onboarding
system.

### Action

Before friend test consider tap stat explanations, one core-loop hint,
Gemheart warning, Intelligence terminology/purpose, and Survive
wording.
