# Shattered Plains --- Idea Vault

Parking lot for possibilities.

**Nothing here is automatically approved.** Contradictory ideas are
allowed.

When an idea becomes real: 1. decide it deliberately, 2. record
rationale in `DESIGN_LEDGER.md`, 3. move implementation work to
`ROADMAP.md`.

# PvP Siege V2

Core fantasy: an asynchronous confrontation rather than "click attack,
math later."

Possible flow: 1. attacker commits encircling force 2. defender receives
warning/reinforces 3. attacker may reinforce 4. attacker chooses when to
assault 5. hard deadline prevents indefinite waiting

Time becomes a resource. Highstorms make waiting dangerous.

Constraint: never make "wait until opponent is offline" optimal.

Future extensions not required for V2: - siege engines - surrender -
prisoners - diplomacy - equipment capture - negotiated withdrawal

# Fourth Bridge

Potential Great Fabrial/flying ship.

Possible identity: - extremely expensive - requires smaller
Fabrials/components - avoids Highstorm exposure - major
logistical/survival benefit

Preference: it should **break a rule**, e.g. "this army is not exposed
to Highstorms," rather than merely add another percentage.

# Persistent Kaiju Chasmfiend

Potential future shared-world boss campaign, separate from the regular
urgent Plateau Run cadence.

A singular, exceptionally powerful Chasmfiend could persist across
multiple encounters, retain wounds between attempts, and become a
seasonal story that requires sustained cooperation to defeat.

Questions: - how long does it remain? - can failed assaults make useful
progress without trivializing the eventual kill? - does it retaliate or
change the world while alive? - how are rewards divided across attempts?
- how does it remain distinct from four independent, time-sensitive
Chasmfiend hunts per day?

Do not add persistence to ordinary Plateau Runs until their immediate
join, contribution, speed, and reward loop proves fun in testing.

# Diplomacy / Trade

Potential direction: low Hostility can support diplomacy/trade while
high Hostility trends toward war economy.

Avoid a generic isolated trading screen.

# Plateau Ownership → Provision Capacity

Possible benefit: territory increases Provision capacity.

Upside: Territory matters economically/militarily.

Risks: - leader snowball - territory loss invalidating armies -
complexity in intentionally simple Provision system

# Capturing Reusable Fabrials

Currently lost reusable Fabrials disappear.

Future PvP possibility: victorious enemy captures lost equipment.

Risks: snowballing, severe feel-bad, farming/exploitation, transfer
complexity.

# Upkeep / Attrition

demonstrated strategic problem.

# Ghostblood Rarity Redesign

Desired fantasy: exceptionally rare, perhaps rarer than Shardbearers.

Possible levers besides price: - availability - Research/Intel
requirements - special opportunities - cooldowns - caps - mission-earned
access - network development

Do not default to absurd pricing.

# Seasonal Scoring Evolution

Current philosophy: interaction over hoarding.

Potential weighting idea: - \~30--40% development - \~60--70%
contest/interaction

Avoid raw Sphere balance or raw army Power as dominant measures.
